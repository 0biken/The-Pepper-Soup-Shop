Betty Harry — The Peppersoup Queen
The face behind the pot

A queen's kitchen, open to everyone.

Betty Harry didn't set out to build a restaurant chain. She set out to share the pepper soup she grew up on — the Kalabari way, made with patience and real ingredients, cooked the way her family always did.

That one-bedroom kitchen became Lekki's most talked-about pepper soup spot, and the story is still being written — one bowl, one guest, one celebration at a time.

"This win is ours. As e dey hot, we go dey serve am — with everything we have."
