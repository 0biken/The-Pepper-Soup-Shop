Betty Harry — The Peppersoup Queen
The woman behind the pot

One kitchen, seven years, one standard.

Betty Harry, the Peppersoup Queen, started cooking the peppersoup she grew up on out of a one-bedroom apartment — the Kalabari way, with patience, real fish and no shortcuts.

Seven years later, that one-bedroom operation caters for Nigeria's biggest weddings, hosts the annual Peppersoup Festival, and serves Lekki Phase 1 from a room built around the culture the food comes from. The pot got bigger. Nothing else changed.

"A love letter to Kalabari culture, Rivers heat, and comfort food."
