The Peppersoup Queen

Betty put culture

Betty Harry began with a camp stove in a one-bedroom apartment. Her love of pepper soup grew into The Pepper Soup Shop and a festival celebrating food, culture and community.

Her work keeps pepper soup at the centre: familiar, deeply spiced and made to bring people together.
