01 — Executive Summary

One platform, every doorway a customer already uses.

Karma Groups proposes a 12 week engagement to design and build one connected platform for ordering, reservations, event bookings, meal subscriptions, customer feedback and staff operations — a responsive website, Android and iOS apps, a structured WhatsApp ordering assistant, and a role based operations application for Betty Harry's team.

Phase One Investment
NGN 5,600,000

Covers design and development of the first production release, deployment support, staff training and 30 days of post launch defect support. Third party usage fees and the V2 roadmap are priced separately.

Where things stand today

Orders already move through WhatsApp, but every conversation needs a person to answer routine questions, collect the same details and relay the request to staff. Reservations, event enquiries, reviews and complaints scatter across individual chats, leaving management with limited visibility once records live in different channels.

What Karma Groups will build

A system that keeps the convenience of WhatsApp while giving The Peppersoup Shop one source of truth for every order, payment, reservation, event request, subscription and customer issue — across web, mobile and chat.

03 — Proposed Solution

Customers choose the channel. Staff work from one record.

One shared backend holds customer information, menus, orders, payments, bookings and status updates — no matter where the order started.

Customer Website

  • Browse food and drinks by category, with images, descriptions, prices, portion information and availability.
  • Customize menu items, select extras and add products to a basket.
  • Choose delivery or pickup and place an immediate or scheduled order.
  • Select an available date and time from a calendar.
  • Pay online and receive confirmation.
  • Create an account, view previous orders and reorder.
  • Reserve a table or submit an event request.
  • Subscribe to an approved meal plan.
  • Submit a review, private complaint or service enquiry.

Android & iOS Applications

A cross platform application delivers the same essential service on Android and iOS without the cost of building two separate native products.

  • Customer registration, login and profile management.
  • Menu browsing, search, favourites, basket and checkout.
  • Saved addresses, order history and quick reordering.
  • Delivery, pickup and scheduled ordering.
  • Reservations, event requests and meal subscriptions.
  • Push notifications for order status, booking confirmation and approved promotions.
  • Customer reviews and feedback.
One shared customer record. Orders placed on the website, mobile applications or WhatsApp all enter the same operations platform — staff never maintain separate order lists per channel.

04 — WhatsApp Ordering & Service Journeys

A guided assistant, with a person always one step away.

The first WhatsApp version uses a structured, menu driven flow — reliable, easy to test, and right for the Phase One budget. Customers can move to a staff member whenever the automated flow no longer fits.

  1. The customer starts a conversation and selects an action.
  2. The assistant presents menu categories and available items.
  3. The customer selects items, quantities and supported options.
  4. The customer chooses delivery, pickup or a scheduled order.
  5. The assistant collects contact and fulfilment details.
  6. The customer receives an order summary and payment link.
  7. The confirmed order enters the operations dashboard.
  8. The customer receives status updates or moves to a staff member when needed.

Table Reservations

  • Date, time, guest count and branch selection.
  • Availability confirmation and deposit where required.
  • Customer confirmation, reminder and cancellation status.
  • A calendar view for authorized staff.

Events & Celebrations

Birthdays, private parties, corporate events, group dining and catering use an enquiry and quotation process, since final pricing depends on guest count, menu, location and service requirements.

  • Event type, preferred date and time, guest count and location.
  • Menu or package preference and special requirements.
  • Management review, quotation and customer approval.

Meal Subscriptions — customers choose approved weekly or monthly plans, select eligible delivery days and manage renewal. Subscription packages and operating rules are confirmed by the business before launch.

05 — Customer Feedback & Business Operations

Every review, complaint and shift, on one screen.

Reviews & Complaints

  • Star ratings and written feedback.
  • Separate ratings for food, service, delivery and ambience where relevant.
  • Reviews linked to completed orders where possible.
  • Optional customer photographs.
  • Administrative moderation and management responses.
  • A private complaint route with assignment, status and resolution records.
  • Approved testimonials selected for public display.

Operations Web Application

Role based access means each staff member sees only the information and actions required for their work.

RolePrimary responsibilities
Super adminSystem settings, user access, roles, menu control, transactions, reports and activity records.
ManagerOrders, payments, reservations, events, customers, subscriptions, reviews, complaints and staff assignment.
Kitchen staffIncoming orders, quantities, preparation notes, scheduled deadlines and preparation status.
Ground staffAssigned orders, table status, guest arrival, service progress, delivery completion and issue escalation.

Central Operating Flow

  1. The customer places an order or submits a booking through the website, app or WhatsApp.
  2. The platform records the request and confirms payment or deposit status.
  3. The operations dashboard routes the request to the appropriate manager or team.
  4. Kitchen or service staff update progress.
  5. The customer receives clear status messages.
  6. The completed transaction becomes available for reporting and customer history.

Client Responsibilities

  • Provide the current menu, prices, descriptions, options and availability rules.

Ownership

After all agreed payments are complete, The Peppersoup Shop will own the custom project deliverables created under the project agreement. Third party services, frameworks and licensed assets remain subject to their own terms.
